# Reels & Shorts Blocker — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

Reels & Shorts Blocker is a browser extension that counts and blocks Instagram Reels and
YouTube Shorts. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

## What it stores
- A count of Reels/Shorts swipes (per platform, per day and hour of day).
- Your settings (daily target, block timer, schedule, toggles).

This data is kept only in your browser's local extension storage
(`chrome.storage.local`). It never leaves your device.

## What it does NOT do
- It does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.
- It makes no network requests and has no server or account system.
- It does not read or store video IDs, titles, URLs, watch history, or any
  page content. It only detects that a Reels/Shorts page is open in order to
  count swipes and show the block screen.
- It uses no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Permissions
- **storage** — saves your counts and settings on your device.
- **Access to instagram.com and youtube.com** — needed to detect Reels/Shorts
  pages and show the counter bubble and block screen.

## Your control
You can export a backup of your data from the extension's Settings tab.
Uninstalling the extension or clearing its data deletes everything.
